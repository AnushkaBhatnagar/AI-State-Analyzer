🚪 The Mysterious Door

An Interactive Adventure

Chapter 1

You're walking home late one evening when you notice something strange. In the alley between two old buildings, there's a door that wasn't there before—a magnificent oak door with intricate carvings and a softly glowing handle.

As you approach, you feel a strange warmth emanating from it. The carvings seem to move in the flickering streetlight, depicting scenes of distant lands and impossible creatures.

What do you do?
